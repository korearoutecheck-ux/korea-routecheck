How prices work

The ranges reflect menu listings checked in September 2026. A single bowl can be inexpensive even when shared dishes or barbecue cost much more.

Quick, affordable meals

Good choices for a simple lunch or dinner near a sightseeing route.

Yongsan₩8,500–₩9,500

Goobok Mandu

Dumplings

A small dumpling shop near Sookmyung Women's University Station. Go for steamed dumplings, pot stickers or xiaolongbao.

Nongmin Baekam Sundae

Well-known local favorites

A little more room in the budget adds several long-running Seoul institutions.

Mapo₩15,000 for the standard bowl

Okdongsik

Pork gomtang

A focused pork-soup restaurant near Hapjeong. Add it to a Hongdae, Yeonnam or Mangwon day and expect a possible wait.

Open in Naver Maps →Michelin Guide
YeouidoMany meals ₩15,000–₩22,000

Jeongin Myeonok

Pyongyang-style cold noodles

A practical meal stop for a Yeouido or Han River day. The mild broth is very different from sweet or spicy cold noodles.

Shared dishes and splurges

Prices that depend on the order

These restaurants can be affordable or expensive depending on whether the table orders a single meal, a shared pot or barbecue.

Pork-bone soup

Open around the clock when checked. Choose a single bowl for one person or a larger pot to share after a Seongsu day.
