About Korea RouteCheck

Seoul plans built from local experience.

Korea RouteCheck is an independent itinerary planner for people visiting Seoul. It combines eight years of living in Korea with current public information from tourism, transport, venue and map sources.

Routes first

Places are grouped by neighborhood so a day remains practical on Seoul's transit system.

Current sources

Hours, prices and access details are linked to official sources or current public listings whenever possible.

Clear limits

The site marks estimates and tells travelers when information must be checked again before visiting.

How recommendations are chosen

Itinerary stops are selected for their relevance to first-time visitors and their fit with nearby places. Restaurant recommendations consider location, menu price, review activity and whether the meal works naturally within the route. A popular place is not included when it would make the day unnecessarily difficult.

How information is checked

Guides link to sources such as Visit Seoul, VisitKorea, venue websites, transport operators, Naver Maps and established guide listings. Pages show when their details were last reviewed. Because Seoul changes quickly, opening hours, reservations, prices and temporary closures should always be confirmed before leaving.

Affiliate independence

Some Viator links are affiliate links. Korea RouteCheck may earn a commission after a qualifying booking, at no extra cost to the traveler. Affiliate availability does not determine the order of itinerary stops or restaurant recommendations.

Privacy

The planner runs in the browser. A generated itinerary can be saved on the device or encoded into a shareable link, but the trip form is not submitted to Korea RouteCheck. Read the full privacy notice.

Corrections and questions

If a place has closed, a price is outdated or a route no longer works well, email korearoutecheck@gmail.com. Include the page and place name so the information can be checked.
