## Facebook or expat/travel group post: airport arrival

The “best” transfer from Incheon Airport depends on the hotel—not just which option reaches Seoul fastest.

AREX Express makes sense for Seoul Station, the all-stop train is often convenient for Hongdae, and an airport bus can be easier when it stops near a hotel in central Seoul, Gangnam or Jamsil. Heavy luggage and late arrivals change the decision again.

## Instagram carousel caption: first day in Seoul

The first day in Seoul begins when the hotel area is reached—not when the flight lands.

A realistic arrival day should account for immigration, baggage, airport transport, luggage storage and jet lag. The easiest plan is usually one flexible neighborhood walk near the accommodation:

• Jongno: Gwanghwamun → Cheonggyecheon → Ikseon-dong
• Myeongdong: shopping streets → Namdaemun edge → dinner
• Hongdae: Yeonnam → Gyeongui Line Forest Park → Hongdae
• Jamsil: Seokchon Lake → Songridan-gil
