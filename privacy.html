Privacy

Privacy at Korea RouteCheck

The planner processes itinerary choices in the visitor's browser. The form is not sent to a Korea RouteCheck server, and no account is required.

Third-party links

Booking and affiliate links lead to third-party websites. Those services have their own privacy, cookie, payment, and data-retention practices.

Optional analytics

Korea RouteCheck may offer an optional analytics choice to understand aggregate page visits, itinerary generations, selected trip lengths, and affiliate-link clicks. Analytics does not load unless the visitor chooses to allow it.

With permission, Google Analytics uses cookies to measure visits and actions such as creating, sharing or booking from a plan. We send the trip length, pace and rain preference with itinerary-generation events. We do not send names, email addresses, itinerary text, lodging amounts, traveler counts or selected interests. Shared itinerary settings are removed from the page URLs sent by our analytics code; campaign tags can be retained to identify referral sources.

Visitors who decline are not tracked by our analytics. Change your choice below at any time. Withdrawing permission stops future collection and reloads this page; it does not erase data already received by Google.

Saved and shared itineraries

The most recent itinerary choices can be stored in the browser on the visitor's device. A shareable itinerary link places the selected trip length, pace, interests, budget style, traveler count and lodging estimate in the URL. Anyone who receives that link can read those choices. No name or email address is included.
