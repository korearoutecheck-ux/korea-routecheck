Privacy

A planner that does not need personal data.

The initial version of Korea RouteCheck processes itinerary selections inside the visitor's browser. The trip form is not submitted to a Korea RouteCheck server, and the site does not require an account.

Third-party links

Booking and affiliate links lead to third-party websites. Those services have their own privacy, cookie, payment, and data-retention practices.

Optional analytics

Korea RouteCheck may offer an optional analytics choice to understand aggregate page visits, itinerary generations, selected trip lengths, and affiliate-link clicks. Analytics does not load unless the visitor chooses to allow it.

The itinerary form continues to run inside the browser. Korea RouteCheck does not send names, email addresses, itinerary text, lodging amounts, or other form entries to analytics. Visitors who decline are not tracked, and a saved preference can be cleared by removing site data in the browser.
